Oceanis 52

What is theOceanis 52?

The Oceanis 52 is a 16.28 m BENETEAU cruising sailing yacht that brings elegance, innovation and heritage together, for the freedom to explore the world's seas in complete safety and comfort.

Naval architecture by Roberto Biscontini, interior and deck by Nauta Design. From 467,500 € excl. VAT.

NominatedEuropean Yacht of the Year 2026
WinnerDesign Innovation Award 2025
Top 10Best Boats Award
LENGTH OVERALL 16.28 M BEAM 4.84 M

Sleek aft lines, a pronounced chine and a full beam carried all the way to the transom.

30% more cockpit.

Twin C-shaped benches form a diamond-shaped space between the companionway and the lifting swim platform, with two height-adjustable tables, a fridge and a plancha.

What is it likebelow deck?

The gently sloping companionway, a signature of Oceanis cruising yachts, leads to a bright saloon with a C-shaped galley, Corian worktops and sanded oak woodwork.
